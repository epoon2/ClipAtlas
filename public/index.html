FROM SAVED TO SCHEDULED

Your itinerary starts here.

Turn saved videos into a draft trip. Overview shows what your clips add up to, city by city, and can fill the days for you; Days is the plan to change as you like; By city lists everything you saved in each city, sorted into food, shopping, sights, activities and places to stay.

This is an ordering board, not an optimized route. Locations and opening hours must be verified before travel.

MAKE IT YOURS

Your categories and tabs.

Sort clips the way you think about them. Sort words decide where a clip goes automatically: a clip whose title, caption, notes or tags contain a category's words is sorted there, and you can always move it.

BUILD THE INDEX

Bring your saves together.

Import a TikTok data export or a spreadsheet you made yourself. Existing notes and collections are preserved when a duplicate link appears.

01 / IMPORT TIKTOK DATA

Choose your export

TikTok: Profile → ☰ → Settings and privacy → Account → Download your data. Choose JSON. Unzip the downloaded file and select the JSON inside.

TikTok's official Favorites export typically contains links and dates. It does not include the original collection-folder assignments or video/audio. You'll add those here.

02 / QUICK CAPTURE

Paste saved video links

One TikTok URL per line. Useful for manually rebuilding a specific collection.

03 / YOUR BACKUP

Keep a portable copy

Download all saved links, manually written details, collections and itinerary days as one JSON backup. Your saved data is synchronized to your ClipAtlas account.

Importing a ClipAtlas backup replaces your library only after confirmation. Ordinary TikTok or CSV imports merge with it instead.

WHAT THE PLANNER KNOWS

Source-first, not guess-first.

A TikTok favorite alone doesn't reveal a restaurant name, location or opening hours. This version supports editing those details and suggesting a category from pasted caption text. It never fabricates addresses or auto-downloads videos.

Let ClipAtlas review your clips

Each clip plays here while it is reviewed.

ClipAtlas plays each clip here, listens to what is said and reads the text on the video, then fills in its title, category, place and tags. Anything you already wrote is kept.

  1. Press Start.
  2. Your browser asks to share this tab: keep This tab and turn on Also share tab audio.
  3. Keep this tab on screen while it works (another window can sit beside it). If you switch away, it pauses and carries on when you come back. The clips play silently.

Tries each step once on a test sound and picture, and shows any error.

    The sound and screenshots go to the AI chosen above, on this site's own accounts. Gemini's free tier takes one request per clip; its daily limit is set by Google (see Google AI Studio) and starts again at midnight Pacific, and Google may use what is sent on the free tier to improve its products. Cloudflare's free allowance (10,000 "neurons" a day, starting again at midnight UTC) covers roughly 30 to 50 clips. The run shows what it used; when both are used up, it stops and carries on from there next time. ClipAtlas does not keep the sound or screenshots, only the details, the transcript and the text read from the video.
