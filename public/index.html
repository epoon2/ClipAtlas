BUILD THE INDEX

Bring your saves together.

Import a TikTok data export or a spreadsheet you made yourself. Existing notes and collections are preserved when a duplicate link appears.

01 / IMPORT TIKTOK DATA

Choose your export

TikTok: Profile → ☰ → Settings and privacy → Account → Download your data. Choose JSON. Unzip the downloaded file and select the JSON inside.

TikTok's official Favorites export typically contains links and dates. It does not include the original collection-folder assignments or video/audio. You'll add those here.

02 / QUICK CAPTURE

Paste saved video links

One TikTok URL per line. Useful for manually rebuilding a specific collection.

03 / YOUR BACKUP

Keep a portable copy

Download all saved links, manually written details, collections and itinerary days as one JSON backup. Your saved data is synchronized to your ClipAtlas account.

Importing a ClipAtlas backup replaces your library only after confirmation. Ordinary TikTok or CSV imports merge with it instead.

WHAT THE PLANNER KNOWS

Source-first, not guess-first.

A TikTok favorite alone doesn't reveal a restaurant name, location or opening hours. This version supports editing those details and suggesting a category from pasted caption text. It never fabricates addresses or auto-downloads videos.
